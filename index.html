  1. Open Gmail settings.
  2. Under Signature, click Create new (or click your current one), then paste.
  3. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and for replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom.

On iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap aA, then Request Desktop Website. Once it is saved, mail you send from the Gmail app carries it too, unless the app has its own signature turned on.
